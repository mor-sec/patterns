AWS AI Pattern Catalogue

Eleven reference architectures for AI solutions on AWS, with Snowflake for data pipelines and prototyping and Neo4j for knowledge graphs where they earn their place. Each pattern is rated for budget, complexity, service level and suitability, so teams can pick the lightest pattern that meets their targets and reviewers can challenge designs consistently.

Cost figures are order-of-magnitude planning ranges, not quotes. Validate with the AWS Pricing Calculator, Snowflake credit tables and Neo4j Aura pricing before committing a budget. Links shown with a dotted underline point to documents in the SAR repository.
